01

ReplyMate

Private build

A multi-account inbox agent for Instagram, WhatsApp and Telegram. It reads the incoming noise, drafts replies in its owner's voice, and sends no text without a human tap. Every approval, edit or rejection becomes training data; the persona refreshes weekly. One server handles many accounts, with signed webhooks and owner-only controls.

02

Polymarket Sentinel

Private build

A paper-first trading desk for Polymarket's five-minute BTC markets. Seven async agents split the work — data, signals, discovery, risk, exits, execution, reporting. New orders pass one risk gate; the live path arms only through an explicit human switch. A read-only dashboard and browser overlay surface the model's edge before anything is clicked.

A 30-day, 8,626-window backtest scored its ≥70% calls at 80–93% by entry minute. Win rate is not edge — the overlay keeps both numbers in view.

PersianWhisper

Open-source study

An open-source Persian speech-to-text app built on Whisper and Gradio. Upload a clip in OGG, WAV or MP3, get a transcript, download it as text. I cloned the project to study its audio pipeline and tokenizer behavior — useful groundwork for Persian voice agents.

Original project by AmirTahaMim / PersianWhisper ↗. Cloned and studied — not my original work.

Heretic

Open-source study

An open-source research tool for automated model steering: directional ablation paired with an Optuna TPE search that trades behavior change against KL drift. I studied the optimization and evaluation loops — especially how it measures what a modification costs, not just what it changes.

Original project by Philipp E. Weidmann / heretic ↗. Cloned and studied — not my original work.
